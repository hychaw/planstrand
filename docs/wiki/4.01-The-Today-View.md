# The Today View

Today is Planstrand's daily workspace, combining **Focus** tasks with **My Day**
scheduling. See [[3.10-Planstrand-V1]] for the current V1.3 RC1 guide.

## What It Shows

**Focus** contains Tasks planned for Today. Use a Task's three-dot menu to plan
it for Today, Tomorrow, This Week, Next Week or a selected date. This Week also
supports flexible work in Anytime This Week. Each Task has one Planning placement;
moving it to another planning date changes that placement.

**My Day** shows the day's schedule, including Task-linked WorkSessions and
Calendar Events. New Tasks created directly in My Day are planned for Today and
appear in Focus. Scheduling an existing Task preserves its Planning assignment.

Task ordering persists across restarts. Context menus offer Move Up/Move Down;
dragging also lets you organize your work.

## Planning, Deadlines and WorkSessions

These are independent:

- **Planning** chooses when you intend to work on a Task and whether it appears
  in today's Focus list.
- **A deadline** records when the Task is due; changing it does not itself assign
  the Task to today's Focus list.
- **WorkSessions** reserve timed periods for a Task. A Task can have multiple
  WorkSessions without changing its Planning assignment.

Unplan removes Planning membership without deleting the Task or its WorkSessions.
A Task can be planned for Today without a WorkSession, or have a WorkSession today
while planned for another day. Completing a WorkSession leaves its parent Task
independent.

Edit WorkSession start and end times to the exact minute from My Day or Calendar;
dragging retains five-minute snapping.

## Folders and Inherited Views

Tasks stay in Inbox or their Folder when you plan or schedule them. Folder
organization, Planning and timed scheduling serve different purposes.

The inherited Today, Planner, Project and Tag views use older work-context
behaviour. Their due-date and logical-day rules do not define membership in the
current Planstrand Focus list.
