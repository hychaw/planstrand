# Planstrand V1.3 RC1

Planstrand is a local-first personal planner. Core planning works offline without
an account. V1.3 RC1 is a Windows x64 prerelease, available as Setup and Portable
packages. See the [release notes](../releases/v1.3.0-rc.1.md) for downloads,
checksums, acceptance results and known limitations.

## Get started

1. Download Setup or Portable from the official release linked above.
2. Create Tasks in Inbox or organize them in hierarchical Folders under **Tasks**.
3. Plan work for Today or This Week.
4. Reserve time with WorkSessions in My Day or Calendar.
5. Add recurring Events or open Weekly Templates from Calendar for reusable routines.

Primary navigation contains **Today**, **This Week**, **Tasks**, and **Calendar**,
with Folder navigation below. Search, Help and Settings remain available.
Inherited tools such as Projects, Tags, Boards, the upstream Planner, plugins and
time tracking remain available through More, subject to their feature settings.
Older wiki pages may describe these inherited workflows rather than current
Planstrand navigation.

## Tasks, Planning and My Day

Tasks supports nested Folders, Inbox and persistent Task ordering. Move Tasks
between Folders through their context menus or drag and drop. Subtasks follow
their top-level Task when it moves to another Folder. Deleting a Folder keeps
its Tasks, whose effective Folder becomes Inbox.

Today combines **Focus** tasks and **My Day** scheduling. Plan Tasks for Today,
Tomorrow, This Week, Next Week or a selected date, or leave them in Anytime This
Week. Navigate previous, current and future planning weeks. Each Task has one
Planning placement; multi-placement remains deferred.

Planning, deadlines and WorkSessions are independent. Unplan removes Planning
membership without deleting the Task or its WorkSessions. New Tasks created
directly in My Day are planned for Today and appear in Focus; scheduling an
existing Task preserves its Planning assignment.

Task context menus provide management, Folder movement and Move Up/Move Down.
Three-dot menus provide Planning and WorkSession scheduling, including for nested
Tasks. Task order persists across restarts.

Schedule multiple WorkSessions for a Task. Edit start and end times to the exact
minute from My Day or Calendar; dragging retains five-minute snapping. Completing
a WorkSession leaves its parent Task independent.

## Calendar and routines

Calendar offers Day, Week, Month and Year views with WorkSessions, local Events
and configured external calendar sources. Calendar weeks follow the configured
week start. Local Events can be timed or all-day and remain independent of Tasks
and WorkSessions.

Recurring Events support daily, weekly and monthly schedules. Edit one occurrence,
this and future occurrences, or the series. Weekly Templates provide reusable
weekly schedules with multiple entries and date bounds. Activated templates appear
in Calendar without creating Tasks or WorkSessions.

Device-local reminders are available for standalone timed Events and WorkSessions:
at the start or 5, 10, 15, 30 or 60 minutes beforehand, with Never to disable them.
Delivery is best effort while Planstrand runs and depends on notification
permissions, sleep and operating-system restrictions. Reliable closed-app delivery
is unavailable. Recurring and all-day Events do not support these reminders.
Reminder preferences are excluded from synchronization and model-only exports.

Blue Thread provides light and dark appearance, Comfortable and Compact density,
and a responsive desktop interface. V1.3 includes British Columbia timezone
compatibility; saved timestamps are preserved, so previously mis-scheduled items
require intentional editing.

## Local data, backups and synchronization

Setup and Portable normally use the same default Windows profile at
`%APPDATA%\Planstrand`, separate from the executable. Portable does not automatically
carry your data between computers.

Before upgrading or deleting a profile, export through **Settings → Sync & Backup
→ Import/Export**. Keep an independent protected copy outside the profile and sync
destination, and preserve credentials and encryption keys separately. Rehearse
restoration in a disposable profile with sync disconnected. Application-data
backups are not complete filesystem-profile backups or automatic rollback.
See [[2.02-Restore-Data-From-Backup]], [[3.06-User-Data]] and the
[backup and restore guide](../v1.2-backup-restore.md).

Synchronization is disabled by default and requires separately configured,
compatible providers and clients. Planstrand provides no hosted SuperSync service;
do not use the upstream hosted service as a Planstrand endpoint. Verify
compatibility before reconnecting existing clients.

## Release and compatibility limits

The public version is **1.3.0-rc.1**; the internal technical version remains
**19.1.0**. V1.3 retains V1.2's IndexedDB schema 12, operation schema 5 and
cross-model version 4.5, with no V1.3 schema migration. Unchanged schemas do not
guarantee rollback. V1.2 profiles cannot be opened by V1.1; rollback requires a
separate pre-upgrade backup/profile.

Windows x64 is the validated distribution platform. Other inherited platform
foundations do not imply equivalent release validation. Packages are unsigned,
and Windows or organizational policies may warn about or block execution.
A V1.2 → V1.3 in-place installer upgrade was not tested for this candidate.

Update discovery points to official Planstrand releases. Installation and upgrades
remain manual; automatic or in-app package downloads, assisted installation and
Restart to Update are unavailable. Invalid TLS certificates are rejected by
default, and updater HTTPS verification remains strict.

Planstrand is an independent MIT-licensed fork of Super Productivity. Upstream
attribution and dependency notices remain applicable; upstream community links
are not a Planstrand release channel.
