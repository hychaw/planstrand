## Optional Synchronization

[[1.02-Configure-Data-Synchronization]] describes inherited sync configuration.
Synchronization is disabled by default. Planstrand provides no hosted SuperSync
service; do not use the upstream hosted service as a Planstrand endpoint.
Verify provider/server and client compatibility before connecting devices.

See [[2.08-Choose-Sync-Backend]], [[2.09-Configure-Sync-Backend]] and
[[3.08-Sync-Integration-Comparison]] for provider guidance. Keep independent
backups: [[2.02-Restore-Data-From-Backup]], [[4.23-Managing-Your-Data]] and
[[3.06-User-Data]] explain recovery and data handling.
